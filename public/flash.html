Everything You Need to Excel

Our end-to-end platform makes remote microcontroller programming, telemetry monitoring, and firmware deployments completely frictionless.

In-Browser ROM Flasher

Write ESP ROM bootloader directly from Chrome or Edge at up to 921,600 baud with zero software or driver installs.

Zero-Touch Wi-Fi Provisioning

Inject Wi-Fi credentials and gateway server endpoints directly over serial without cumbersome captive portals.

Live Hardware Telemetry

Track real-time Wi-Fi signal strength (RSSI), free heap memory, device uptime, and heartbeat ping latency.

Multi-Chip Architecture

Plug-and-play identification for ESP32 DevKit, WROOM, ESP8266 NodeMCU, ESP32-S3, and RISC-V ESP32-C3.

Anti-Brick Fail-Safe

Automatic dual-partition rollback and cryptographic MD5 integrity verification protect your boards from faulty builds.

Remote Serial Debugging

Stream live serial logs, sensor values, and remote debug traces straight to your browser console across the globe.

Centralized Fleet Cockpit

Monitor all registered boards, deploy updates, and remotely reboot microcontrollers or test LEDs in one unified view.

Frictionless Friend Sharing

Send a simple web link to friends or collaborators so they can link their hardware into your fleet in under 60 seconds.

Zero-Config Edge Tunnel

Built-in encrypted edge tunnels eliminate port-forwarding, static IPs, and router firewall headaches completely.
