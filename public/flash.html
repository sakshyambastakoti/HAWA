How In-Browser Flashing Works

🔌

1. Plug USB Cable

Connect any ESP32 or ESP8266 board to your Mac, Windows, or Linux laptop using a standard micro-USB or USB-C data cable.

⚡

2. In-Browser Bootloader

Using native Web Serial API, Hawa connects directly to the Silicon Labs / CH340 chip and runs the esptool ROM flasher inside Chrome or Edge.

🌐

3. Instant Fleet Sync

Wi-Fi credentials and encryption keys are injected via serial handshake. Your board boots up, connects to Wi-Fi, and appears on the Hawa dashboard.

Hardware Compatibility

Supported Microcontrollers

ESP32 DevKit & WROOM

Dual-core Tensilica LX6 at 240MHz, 520KB SRAM, 4MB Flash, integrated 2.4GHz 802.11 b/g/n Wi-Fi & Bluetooth.

ESP8266 NodeMCU & D1 Mini

Single-core 32-bit RISC processor at 80/160MHz with full TCP/IP stack. Lightweight, ubiquitous, and reliable.

ESP32-S3 & ESP32-C3

Next-generation hardware crypto acceleration, native USB OTG, and RISC-V architectures with high power efficiency.
